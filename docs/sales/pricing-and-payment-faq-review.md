## Review checklist
- [ ] Confirm or rewrite #5 (payments), #6 (ownership) with real, founder-approved terms.
- [ ] Sanity-check every other answer for tone and accuracy.
- [ ] Ensure no answer over-commits legally (delivery guarantees, refund terms, SLAs) without sign-off.
- [ ] Publish approved FAQs (`is_published = true`). Leave any still-under-review answer unpublished — the page and schema simply omit it.

Publishing wording that promises payment terms, ownership, or guarantees is a founder/legal decision, not an engineering one.
